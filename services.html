Specialized Services

Unique clinical expertise

Specialized in the clinical and forensic care of individuals with a history of incarceration. Bridging lived experience with professional expertise to support transformation and reentry.

  • Sentenced to 24 years in prison as a teenager; served over 16 years incarcerated.
  • Lived experience of juvenile incarceration, county jail, and multiple prisons across California.
  • Designed and facilitated rehabilitative programming for the incarcerated population while inside prison.
  • Years of intensive work with transitional-aged youth on probation in a collaborative court program.
  • Designs and runs rehabilitative curriculum for adults in prisons across the country, and reentry groups.
  • Provides individual psychotherapy for individuals sentenced to life in prison preparing for the parole board.
  • Doctoral capstone: ‘Person-centered Smart Decarceration,’ an MSW course centered on community members with lived experience of incarceration.
